## 1. Foundational & Epistemic Audit Prompts

Use these prompts to audit the factual grounding and evidence boundaries of the research corpus:

1. **Evidence vs. Assumption Audit:**
   > *"What assumptions about LACE are actually supported by empirical evidence, and which ones are currently just unproven hypotheses? Cite specific reports for each."*

2. **Epistemic Classification:**
   > *"Separate every major claim across the LACE corpus into FACT, EVIDENCE, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, and DECISION based on our research principles."*

3. **Evolution & Historical Pivots:**
   > *"Which conclusions or architectural decisions changed over time, and what specific empirical findings or failures caused those shifts?"*

4. **Internal Contradiction Analysis:**
   > *"Where do our research documents contradict each other, show tension in definitions, or reflect unresolved disagreements between team tracks?"*

5. **Unvalidated Blind Spots:**
   > *"What core aspects of the LACE product thesis and recovery model remain completely unvalidated by empirical data?"*
